WildSlabs / Collector service

Collector information.

Essential details for viewing, reserving and caring for a limited WildSlabs object. Operational policies can be connected directly to WooCommerce during the final WordPress build.

01 / Shipping

Handled with care.

Each object is prepared for tracked, insured dispatch in protective presentation packaging. Final delivery windows, destination rates and tax handling will be configured in WooCommerce before launch.

  • DispatchEdition availability and packing are confirmed before collection.
  • TrackingA shipment reference is supplied after dispatch.
  • InternationalAny destination duties will be shown or clarified in the final store policy.

02 / Authenticity

Provenance included.

WildSlabs releases are presented as numbered, limited objects. Every production item should be condition checked and paired with a matching certificate and catalogue record before fulfilment.

  • EditionEdition size and remaining availability are recorded on each object page.
  • CertificateA numbered certificate accompanies the physical object.
  • RecordMaterials, dimensions and object code remain attached to the product entry.

03 / Journal

Stories behind the objects.

The WildSlabs journal is reserved for release notes, cultural references, studio process and collector features. WordPress CMS hooks will allow each entry to be published without changing the front-end code.

04 / Terms

Prototype purchase note.

This HTML/CSS/JavaScript build demonstrates the intended shopping journey. The current reservation form does not process payment. Final payment, privacy, cancellation, returns, taxes and jurisdiction terms must be approved and published before the live WordPress/WooCommerce launch.

  • PaymentConnect an approved WooCommerce gateway over SSL.
  • ReturnsPublish a policy appropriate to limited-edition and made-to-order items.
  • PrivacyAdd the final privacy and cookie notices before collecting customer data.
